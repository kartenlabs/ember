Ambient-audio strip that sits under the timer — the radio half of the product.

```jsx
<NowPlaying track="rain on the window — side b" playing onToggle={…} onMute={…} />
```

- Carries the scanline texture; it is one of only two places that texture appears.
- Transport is two small icon buttons. No seek bar, no track list, no artwork.
- Track names stay lowercase.
