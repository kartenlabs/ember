# Ember identity

## Positioning

**A quiet pomodoro timer with an ambient soundtrack for work.**

Ember combines a clear work/break rhythm, adjustable chimes, an optional ambient station, and a warm pixel interface. It is a small place to settle into a session, with no account required and settings and logs stored in the browser.

**Product:** ember · **Company:** kartenlabs · **Company social:** @kartenlabs.

Use “Ember” at the start of sentences and in editorial prose. Use the supplied lowercase-source pixel wordmark for the visual identity. Silkscreen's glyph shapes naturally resemble pixel capitals; do not replace them with a different uppercase font.

## The ambient station as a differentiator

Feature the music as part of the product experience. The enduring promise is a timer with an optional soundtrack; the current station is a supporting detail that can change.

Preferred copy:

> A quiet timer. An optional soundtrack for your session.

> Currently featuring Claude FM — music for thinking and building — by Claude, embedded via YouTube.

“For focus” or “for a working session” describes intent. “Improves concentration,” “scientifically proven,” “boosts productivity,” and quantified benefits would require evidence the project does not currently have. The stream is publicly available; do not describe it as exclusive to Ember or as an AI integration. There is no Claude model integration in the timer.

When Claude FM is prominent, include “Ember is an independent kartenlabs project.” Longer descriptions should state that Ember is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. Do not use Claude/Anthropic logos as Ember brand elements, download the stream, or upload its audio as an Instagram soundtrack without separate rights.

## Marks and backgrounds

| Asset | Surface | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cream wordmark | Ink/night | Website hero, dark slides, dark photographs with sufficient contrast |
| Ink wordmark | Cream/Daylight | Light slides, press documents |
| Amber wordmark | Ink/night | Focus-themed campaign emphasis |
| Amber e avatar | Supplied opaque amber square | Product profile imagery, app icon |
| Night/Daylight e avatar | Supplied opaque square | Alternate product avatars |

The logo masters are SVG paths and need no installed font. Keep the aspect ratio. Do not stretch, rotate, add gradients, bevels, glow, rounded containers, or drop shadows. The e avatar comes from the wordmark's existing letterform.

Keep clear space at least half the wordmark's visible cap height on every side. At small sizes, use the e avatar instead of shrinking the whole wordmark below approximately 120 px in a social graphic. Check at the actual displayed size. Avatars have generous internal padding for platform circle crops; the source artwork remains square.

Amber, sage, and clay carry mode meaning. Use ink text on the accent fills. For small text on a light surface, use ink or a darker semantic accent, not pale amber. These values follow `design-system/tokens`; the brand kit does not change the app's tokens.

## Type and composition

- **Silkscreen:** wordmark, countdowns, brief overlines. Never long body copy.
- **Instrument Sans:** headlines, sentences, explanations.
- **IBM Plex Mono:** URLs, credits, measurements, metadata.

Use strong differences in type size, generous empty space, and square-edged panels. Align to a 4 px grid. Hard shadows are appropriate for an isolated timer panel; decorative glow and gradients are not. Sound-bar artwork is illustrative. Screen patterns should remain subtle enough to compress cleanly.

The export kit bundles the app's Latin font subsets with their OFL licenses. Other scripts/languages may need full font files and a new export. SVG marks are outlined; HTML presentations and PDFs retain readable text elsewhere.

## Voice

Short, factual, warm, unhurried. Speak from the company account in social copy. Avoid hype, guilt, exclamation marks, emoji, invented testimonials, or promises about output. Favor session, focus, break, chime, set, and log.
