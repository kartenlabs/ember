## Publishing

Upload PNGs directly rather than taking screenshots of the gallery. Add the supplied alt text. Check tiny labels on a phone preview. Keep all carousel slides in sequence. A URL printed in a graphic is not clickable; include the link in the accompanying post, profile, Story sticker, or document description.

The Story's main message and website sit in the central region. The small top and bottom branding is secondary and can be covered by platform controls. Actual control positions vary by device.

Use the company cover on the **kartenlabs Page as an Ember campaign**, with the existing kartenlabs profile logo. No personal-profile cover is supplied because these materials are intended for the company account.

## Reference sources

LinkedIn's official [Page image specifications](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a563309/image-specifications-for-your-linkedin-pages-and-career-pages) retrieved for this kit list 4200 × 700 for the Page cover, 1200 × 627 for custom link-post images, and a 3 MB limit for Page image assets. A compact alternate is also included because platform layouts and upload flows change; the upload screen is the final crop check.

Instagram's [photo resolution help](https://help.instagram.com/1631821640426723) was rate-limited during preparation. The Instagram sizes are deliberate 4:5, 1:1, and 9:16 production exports, not a claim about the maximum supported dimensions or current feed treatment.

Google's [software application structured-data guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/software-app) describes eligibility and validation. The metadata in this kit does not guarantee a rich result, search position, or indexing.
