# Attribution and distribution

## Ember graphics

The kit extends the existing Ember identity for kartenlabs. The wordmark and monogram are outlines of the app's Silkscreen lettering. They are typographic assets, not a commissioned third-party logo. No trademark clearance or corporate trademark registration is represented by this kit.

The app's original design brief, token palette, voice, and type roles remain the visual reference. The e avatar is a new compact use of the existing letterform for the asset kit. The application logo itself is not changed.

## Fonts

The offline HTML files embed the app's Latin subsets of Silkscreen, IBM Plex Mono, and Instrument Sans. Copies are in `07-source/fonts`. Their SIL Open Font License texts are in this folder and should travel with source/font distributions.

Sources: [Silkscreen](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/silkscreen), [IBM Plex Mono](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/ibmplexmono), [Instrument Sans](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/instrumentsans). The exported SVG wordmarks use paths, not font binaries or live text. Other graphic text remains text in the HTML/PDF exports.

## Product icons

The captured app contains Pixelarticons by Gerrit Halfmann. `Pixelarticons-MIT.txt` is included. No icon pack is rebranded as Ember's original work.
